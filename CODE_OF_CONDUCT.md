# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Everyone in project spaces agrees to be respectful, use welcoming language, accept constructive feedback, and criticize ideas rather than people. Respect privacy and accessibility needs, and give others room to learn.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content directed at others, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For non-sensitive incidents, contact a maintainer through GitHub project spaces. For a private initial report, use [GitHub's private reporting channel](https://github.com/shardapps/clippboard/security/advisories/new). Do not post third-party private information publicly. This channel also handles security reports until a dedicated community contact is established.

Maintainers handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to investigate. They may remove content, request changes, warn, temporarily restrict, or ban participants for severe/repeated violations. Enforcement should be proportionate. Concerns about a maintainer may be raised with another Shard Apps maintainer through the same private channel.

This policy covers project-managed spaces and public representation of the project.
